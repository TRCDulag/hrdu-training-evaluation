# What's new

## 1.1.0 (Oct 30, 2026)

- The app is now the HRDU System, with a second module: Leave cards.
- Each employee's leave card under the CSC Omnibus Rules on Leave: 1.25 days of VL and SL earned every month, less 1/30 for each day without pay; tardiness and undertime charged to VL with the CSC conversion table; sick leave going to VL when SL runs out; special privilege leave up to 3 days a year; monetization with the 0.0481927 factor; forced leave not taken deducted at year end; a terminal leave estimate.
- Leave applications on CS Form No. 6 (Revised 2020), printed on long bond paper like HRDU's own sheet, with drop-down lists for who certifies, recommends and approves (Signatories, on the Accounts page). An approved application goes on the leave card.
- Employees get their own sign-in. They see only their own leave card, apply for leave, and print their application. HRDU can make accounts for everyone at once and print the password slips.

## 1.0.2 (Oct 29, 2026)

- The app also opens from GitHub Pages (trcdulag.github.io/hrdu-training-evaluation). The participants' links and QR codes now point there, so they open even on a browser signed in to several Google accounts.
